# Brand guide: Acme Rostering (MADE-UP EXAMPLE)

This is a fictional company. Every quote, claim, and source below is invented for practice.

## 1. Positioning

Shift managers at multi-site cafes use Acme Rostering to build next week's roster in minutes. Unlike spreadsheets, it flags award and break rules before you publish.

## 2. Audience

| Role | Main problem (their words) | Source | Fear about changing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buyer (owner) | "I find out we broke a break rule when the staff member complains." | Made-up sales call 3 | Staff push back on a new app |
| User (shift manager) | "Sunday nights are rostering night. I hate it." | Made-up review 7 | Learning another tool |

## 3. Voice traits

| Trait | This | Not that | Real example line | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plain | Short words a tired manager reads in one go | Industry jargon | "Build next week's roster before your coffee goes cold." | Made-up homepage |
| Warm | Talks like a manager who has done the job | Cheerful hype | "We've done the Sunday roster too. It's why we built this." | Made-up founder post |
| Specific | Names the shift, the rule, the moment | Vague promises | "Flags a missed 30-minute break before you hit publish." | Made-up product page |

## 4. Words

| Use | Avoid | Why we avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| roster | workforce optimisation | Jargon our buyers never use |
| shift | resource | Cold and abstract |
| quick | revolutionary | Hype we cannot prove |

## 5. Claims

| Claim | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Flags break rules before publishing | Made-up product page | sourced |
| "Saves 5 hours a week" | none | needs source |
| "The #1 rostering app" | none | do not use |

## 6. Offer

- What the buyer gets: a 14-day free trial (made-up offer).
- What they do next: start the trial and import last week's roster.

## 7. CTA style

- CTAs in use: "Try it free", "Build your roster".
- Length: 2 to 3 words.
- Tone: direct, no exclamation marks.

## 8. Never say

1. "Revolutionise your workforce."
2. "Unlock the power of scheduling."
3. "Seamless."
4. "Best-in-class."
5. "Say goodbye to rostering forever."
6. "AI-powered magic."
7. "Game-changing."
8. "Effortless compliance."
9. "Join thousands of happy customers." (no source)
10. Anything with more than one exclamation mark.
