## Layout

- Logo position: top left, small (source-declared)
- CTA position: bottom left, marigold button (source-declared)

## Photo and illustration style

- Subjects: a cafe shift manager at the counter with a phone, or a phone screen showing a roster
- Lighting: warm, early morning
- Framing: close to medium, room on the right for the product
- People: real-looking staff in aprons, no stock-photo smiles

## Logo rules

- Versions: paper on navy, navy on paper
- Clear space: the height of the "A" on every side
- Minimum size: 24 px tall
- Placement: top left only

## Never do

1. No gradients behind text.
2. No more than one marigold element besides the CTA.
3. No exclamation marks.
4. No clocks, calendars, or hourglass icons.
5. No office desks or laptops. The setting is a cafe.
6. No text over a face.
