# How to find competitor ads

Both libraries are free and public. You do not need an ad account to search them.

## Meta Ad Library (Facebook and Instagram)

1. Go to facebook.com/ads/library.
2. Pick a country, or All.
3. Set the ad category to All ads.
4. Type the competitor's brand name. Pick their Page from the dropdown, so you see their ads and not keyword matches.
5. Use Filters to show active ads only.
6. Note each ad's "Started running on" date.
7. Screenshot 10 to 20 ads, or copy each ad's text and headline. Include the oldest active ads, since advertisers tend to keep running what works.

## LinkedIn Ad Library

1. Go to linkedin.com/ad-library.
2. Search by the competitor's company name.
3. Open each ad to see its copy and format.
4. Screenshot or copy 10 to 20 ads.

LinkedIn matters most for B2B (business to business) brands. Check both libraries for the same competitor.

## What to capture for each ad

- The full copy and headline.
- The format: image, video, carousel, document, or text.
- The start date, if shown.
- The landing page, if you can click through.
- The library and the date you captured it.

## Rules

- Public ad libraries show what ran, not how it performed.
- Remove nothing from the ad. Add your notes separately.
- Keep captures in one folder per competitor per month, so you can compare over time.
