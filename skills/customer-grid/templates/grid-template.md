## Awareness stages

From Eugene Schwartz, Breakthrough Advertising. Not sure? Start at Problem aware. The page repeats what the ad promised. Part 1 named four pages, so Product aware and Most aware share one.

| Stage | They know | The page should be |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Nothing yet | A creator page that tells a story |
| Problem aware | They have the problem | A problem page in their words |
| Solution aware | Solutions exist | A proof page, such as expert or clinician proof |
| Product aware | Your product exists | A product page with the offer |
| Most aware | They want it | A product page with the offer |
