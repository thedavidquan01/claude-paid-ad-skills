# Ad copy rubric

Score each line from 1 to 5. The total is out of 25.

| Line | What a 1 looks like | What a 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Specificity | Any competitor could paste it into their ad | A detail only this product could claim |
| Customer language | Marketing speak | Words lifted from real customer quotes |
| One clear idea | Two or more ideas fight for attention | The reader can repeat the point after one read |
| Credible claim | A claim with no proof (always a 1) | Every claim traces to the Proof line or a `sourced` claim |
| Hook | This audience scrolls past | This audience stops scrolling |

## Length guides

Check current limits in each platform's help pages before you launch. Platforms change them.

- Meta and LinkedIn: keep the hook in the first line. Most feeds cut long primary text behind a "see more" link.
- Google responsive search ads: headlines up to 30 characters and descriptions up to 90 characters, per Google Ads help at the time of writing.

## Claims check labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `backed by brief` | The brief or brand guide holds the proof |
| `needs proof` | Could be true, but no proof was given |
| `remove` | Not provable, or breaks the brand guide |
| `needs compliance review` | Regulated category flag. A qualified person decides |
